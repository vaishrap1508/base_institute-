# Badges

Learn how to unlock achievements, display trophies inside your profile carousel, and showcase milestones.

---

### What You'll Learn

After reading this guide, you will understand:
✓ Finding locked and unlocked badges on your profile  
✓ Checking badge criteria and progress bars  
✓ Displaying badges to recruiter search feeds  
✓ Unlocking special company certification badges  

---

## Why Badges Exist

A profile detailing "solved 400 questions" is less engaging than a showcase of verified achievements. **Badges** act as trophies verifying that you have completed specific milestones under timed pressure:
- **Trophy Case**: A horizontal scroll carousel on your profile dashboard.
- **Milestone Tracking**: Locked badges are grayed out, displaying a progress bar and the exact unlock requirement.

---

## Core Achievement Badges

Unlock these starting milestones:

### 1. First Step (Getting Started)
- **Unlock**: Complete your first learning activity, quiz, or practice solve on the platform.

### 2. Early Bird (Consistency)
- **Unlock**: Study on the platform for 3 consecutive days.

### 3. Ratio Master (Quantitative)
- **Unlock**: Solve 25 quantitative ratio questions with an overall accuracy of `>= 80%`.

### 4. TCS Crusher (Company Specific)
- **Unlock**: Pass 3 TCS NQT mock tests with a qualifying score.

---

## Showcase Your Milestones

Recruiters review your Trophy Case to verify:
- **Discipline**: High-level consistency badges (e.g. `Early Bird`, `Consistency King`).
- **Subject Mastery**: Concept-specific badges (e.g. `Ratio Master`, `Syllogism Sage`).
- **Exam Readiness**: Company-specific mock test qualifiers (e.g. `TCS Crusher`).

---

## Badges Best Practices

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Review Locked badge criteria**  
  Regularly open your profile page and hover over the grayed-out badges. Pick a badge that is at 75% progress (e.g., *20/25 questions solved for Ratio Master*) and focus your next practice session on that topic.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: Certification Locks**  
  Mock test certification badges are locked until academic credential forms are completed. Ensure your CGPA and college names are verified.
</Callout>
