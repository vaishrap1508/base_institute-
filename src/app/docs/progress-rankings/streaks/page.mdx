## Why Streaks Matter

Placement preparation requires long-term habit loops. Cramming 10 hours before an exam yields lower retention than studying 20 minutes daily. The **Streak System** monitors your daily activity to reward steady, incremental progress:
- **Daily Solve**: Completing a single question in the Practice Arena sets your active status for the day.
- **Visual Indicator**: A fire flame icon on the dashboard header displays your active streak count.

---

## Streak Active Windows and Calculations

```
[ Day 1: Solve Question ] ──► [ Day 2: Within 24-48 hours ] ──► [ Streak Increments ]
                                      │
                                      └──► [ Exceeds 48 hours ] ➔ [ Streak Decays to 0 ]
```

### 1. Streak Increment Window
- **Rule**: If `now() - last_activity_at < 24 hours` (relative to Indian Standard Time - IST), the streak is maintained. If it exceeds 24 but is within 48 hours, it increments.

### 2. Streak Freeze Shields
Purchase freeze shields in the Shop or earn them through achievements:
- **Automatic Trigger**: If you miss a 48-hour solving window, a freeze shield is consumed automatically to protect your streak.
- **Limit**: You can hold a maximum of 2 freeze shields at any time.

## Consistency Best Practices

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Solve a "Casual" question on busy days**  
  If you are busy with university tasks, solve a single **Easy** question in the Practice Arena. This takes under 60 seconds and keeps your streak alive while protecting your freeze shields.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: Reset Penalties**  
  If your streak decays to 0, your global consistency percentile index on the leaderboards drops, which recruiters use to gauge candidate discipline. Protect your streak!
</Callout>
