## Why XP & Levels Exist

Aptitude study is a marathon. Experience Points (XP) and Levels break this long preparation cycle into milestones:
- **Actions Value**: Every correct solve, completed folder revision, and mock test score contributes XP.
- **Progress Tracking**: Your Level indicates your preparation maturity. A Level 30 candidate has solved an order of magnitude more questions than a Level 5 rookie.

---

## XP Allocation Values

Perform these tasks to accumulate XP:

| Task | XP Reward | Modifier / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solve Easy Question** | +10 XP | Core formula correctness |
| **Solve Medium Question** | +20 XP | Mixed logical calculation |
| **Solve Hard Question** | +40 XP | Tricky shortcuts and complex puzzles |
| **Complete Revision Folder** | +100 XP | Clear all items inside a folder |
| **Pass Mock Exam** | +250 XP | CompleteTimed Qualifiers |
| **Daily Streak Active** | +15 XP / day | Consecutive logins multiplier |

---

## Level Milestones and Rank Titles

Your Level unlocks titles on your Placement Profile:
- **Level 1 to 9 (Rookie)**: Title `Ratio Rookie`. Standard quantitative basics.
- **Level 10 to 19 (Scholar)**: Title `Formula Finder`. Core logical arrangement familiarity.
- **Level 20 to 29 (Master)**: Title `Logic Legend`. High solving speed and balanced domains.
- **Level 30+ (Grandmaster)**: Title `Aptitude Sage`. Certified placement ready.

## Gamification Best Practices

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Solve Harder Problems for fast level-ups**  
  If you are close to leveling up, solve **Hard** questions or complete a mock test. These yield double and quadruple the XP of easy questions, boosting your rank card.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: XP decay is not active**  
  Unlike streaks, your XP never decays. It represents your lifetime effort on the platform. Keep practicing to climb the global levels leaderboard.
</Callout>
