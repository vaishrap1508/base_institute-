## Why Leaderboards Exist

Peer motivation increases preparation quality. The **Global Leaderboard** displays student progress transparently, helping you compare your solving volume and consistency against other candidates:
- **Global Rank**: Mapped by your aggregate correct solves and level metrics.
- **Percentile Indicator**: Displays your standing (e.g. *Top 12%*) relative to the total active candidate pool.

---

## Leaderboard Calculation Metrics

Ranking indexes are determined by:

$$\text{Ranking Score} = \text{Correct Solves} \times 10 + \text{Streak Count} \times 25 + \text{Level} \times 50$$

### 1. Correct Solves
- **Weight**: Represents basic problem-solving volume.

### 2. Consistency Bonus
- **Weight**: Multiplies your active daily streak. High streaks prevent ranking drops when solve volumes are equal.

### 3. Graduation Batch Filters
Filter comparisons by graduation batch (e.g. *2027 graduates*) or specific college departments to see your rank inside your immediate target cohort.

## Leaderboard Best Practices

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Focus on Percentile brackets**  
  Do not worry about hitting the global #1 spot. Recruiters filter candidates by brackets (e.g. *Top 10%*). Hitting the top 10% bracket is sufficient to qualify for premium recruiter selections.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: RLS Policy Protection**  
  Your rank card and name are visible publicly only if your profile visibility is set to `Public` or `Friends Only`. Private accounts are anonymous on public lists.
</Callout>
