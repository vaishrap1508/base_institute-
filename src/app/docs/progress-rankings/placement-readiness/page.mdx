## Why Placement Readiness Exists

Many students solve hundreds of questions but fail company qualifiers because they have selective blindspots (e.g. high quantitative accuracy but very slow solve speeds). 

The **Placement Readiness Index (PRI)** compiles your correctness, speed, consistency, and mock exam metrics into a single, comprehensive indicator representing your mock-test and placement-qualifying readiness.

---

## The Readiness Radar Chart

Your PRI is displayed as a shaded pentagon charting five coordinates:

```
          [ Quantitative (Math) ]
                  /     \
    [ Solve Speed ]     [ Accuracy ]
          |                 |
    [ Verbal Skill ] — [ Logical Skill ]
```

### 1. Accuracy Coordinate
- **Calculation**: Overall correctness rate across your last 50 attempts.
- **Benchmark**: Service target `= 70%`, Product target `= 85%`.

### 2. Solve Speed Coordinate
- **Calculation**: Average time to submit correct answers.
- **Benchmark**: Target `< 80 seconds` per item.

### 3. Subject Coordinates (Quant, Logical, Verbal)
- **Calculation**: Mastery percentage based on subtopics completed in the roadmap.

---

## Readiness Levels and Target Cutoffs

Your readiness score dictates your profile's placement indicator:

| Readiness Score | Index Level | Target Placements | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **80% to 100%** | **Gold Tier** | Product companies, premium IT rounds | Start mock phase immediately |
| **60% to 79%** | **Silver Tier** | Service recruiters (TCS Digital, Cognizant) | Balance solve speed in Practice Arena |
| **Below 60%** | **Bronze Tier** | Foundations phase | Review concept formulas, do basic practice |

## Example Student Journey

> **Vaishnavi's Readiness Boost**  
> Vaishnavi is preparing for placements. Her radar chart displays:
> - Quantitative: 82%
> - Verbal: 58% (Weakness)
> - Solve Speed: 74%
> - Accuracy: 78%
>
> To lift her overall PRI to the Gold Tier, she stops practicing quantitative questions and focuses on Verbal subtopics.
> - She solves 30 grammar error-spotting questions.
> - Her verbal coordinate increases to 76%, lifting her overall PRI to 81% and qualifying her profile for differential recruitment listings.

---

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Balance the Pentagon**  
  Do not let any single coordinate drop below 60%. A balanced PRI at 75% across all five axes is more attractive to recruiter filters than a profile with 95% Quantitative but 40% Verbal.
</Callout>
