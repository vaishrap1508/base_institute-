## Anatomy of the Placement Profile

Your profile page is not just a form. It acts as an **Aptitude Resume** that demonstrates your quantitative, logical, and verbal problem-solving capacity.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Avatar + Effect]  Name @username                           |
|                    Level 27 - "Logic Legend"                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Aptitude DNA Stats]                                        |
| Solves: 1,240      Accuracy: 88.5%    Streak: 42 Days       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Placement Readiness Radar]    [Activity Heatmap]           |
| SVGs detailing strengths       GitHub-style 53-week log     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Trophy Case]                  [Academic Credentials]       |
| Locked/Unlocked badges         University & Branch details   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## Key Profile Features

### 1. Avatar Accessories & Customization
- **Frames**: Personalize your appearance with Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Neon frames.
- **Rank Borders**: Dynamic visual containers (e.g. Diamond, Top 100) that glow and spin depending on your global percentile.
- **Visual Effects**: Particle animations, fire aura, electric rings, and cosmic glow effects that layer over your image.

### 2. Aptitude DNA Stat Grid
- **Total Solves**: Completed problem counts mapped against the platform's career path benchmarks.
- **Accuracy Rate**: Clean SVG concentric rings displaying correctness ratios per domain.
- **Consistency Index**: Track consecutive active days. Keeping consistency alive prevents streak decays.

### 3. Placement Readiness Radar
An interactive pentagonal coordinate chart assessing five dimensions:
- **Quantitative**: Percentage margins and algebra speeds.
- **Logical**: Seating matrix sequencing and syllogisms.
- **Verbal**: Grammar check logic and comprehension.
- **Solve Speed**: Average time elapsed per correct answer.
- **Accuracy**: General correctness rates across all attempted domains.

## Best Practices for Profile Setup

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Public vs Recruiter Visibility**  
  Keep your profile visibility settings set to `Recruiter Visible` or `Public`. This allows partners and recruitment managers to search your stats, review your readiness index, and reach out for interviews.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: Credential Accuracy**  
  Always enter accurate college names, branches, CGPA, and graduation years. These credentials are cross-referenced during mock test certifications and company recruitment matches.
</Callout>
