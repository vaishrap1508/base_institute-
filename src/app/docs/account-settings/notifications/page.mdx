## Why Notification Settings Matter

Aptitude learning schedules decay if not maintained regularly. Notifications keep you aware of critical events without causing inbox clutter.

---

## Stored Notification Channels

Configure these three notification channels:

### 1. Streak Warning Emails
- **Purpose**: Dispatched automatically if 36 hours have elapsed since your last practice solve, alerting you to update your streak before decay.

### 2. Mock Test Scheduling Alerts
- **Purpose**: Dispatched 24 hours before a scheduled college mock test begins, including syllabus topics.

### 3. Weekly AI Insights
- **Purpose**: Delivers a digest of your weekly readiness index changes, highlighting your top two improvements.

## Notification Settings Best Practices

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Keep Streak Reminders Active**  
  Do not unsubscribe from streak alerts. Hitting a 42-day consistency index is difficult; a simple email reminder can help protect your statistics on busy days.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: Third-Party Email Blockers**  
  Ensure that verification and test schedule emails are not captured by university spam filters. Add `verify@aptitudemaster.edu` to your contacts whitelist.
</Callout>
