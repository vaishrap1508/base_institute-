## Why Priority Management Exists

A common issue in placement preparation is reviewing simple questions over and over while ignoring the complex ones. **Priority flags** keep you focused on the problems that challenge you:
- **🔴 High Priority**: Reserved for questions where you made a fundamental logical error or struggled to set up equations.
- **🟡 Medium Priority**: Questions solved correctly, but where you took more than 120 seconds.
- **🟢 Low Priority**: Foundational questions saved for formula records.

---

## Interactive Priority Selector

Priority can be edited instantly in 3 locations:

```
[ Active Question ]
     ├──► Breadcrumb Badge: click to cycle (🔴 ➔ 🟡 ➔ 🟢)
     ├──► Quick Save Drawer: toggle circles in right panel
     └──► Quick Notes: edit button in bottom overlay popup
```

---

## The Today's Revision Queue

Marking questions as `🔴 High Priority` automatically injects them into the **Today's Revision Queue** inside the Revision Library.
- **Visual Callout**: An inline `Flame` icon flags these items.
- **Order**: Pinned to the top of the feed to ensure they are the first items reviewed when study sessions begin.

---

## Priority Management Best Practices

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Downgrade Priority systematically**  
  When re-solving High Priority items during weekly reviews:
  - If you solve it correctly in under 60 seconds, downgrade it to `🟡 Medium`.
  - If you solve it instantly without checking hints, downgrade to `🟢 Low` or remove the bookmark.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: Flag Inflation**  
  Do not mark every question as High Priority. If more than 30% of your bookmarked questions are High Priority, the Revision Library feed becomes cluttered. Reserve High Priority for true logical bottlenecks.
</Callout>
