## Why Video Solutions Exist

Text solutions often hide the small visual clues that experienced trainers use to solve questions quickly. **Video Solutions** walk you through the problem-solving logic step-by-step:
- **Visual Clues**: See the trainer draw seating outlines or group algebraic equations.
- **Mental Shorthand**: Hear the explanations of *why* specific steps are skipped to save time.

Rather than watching a full 15-minute video, you can jump directly to the specific timestamp for the step where you got stuck.

## Timeline Jump-to-Timestamp Navigation

Each video contains annotated time links:

```
[ 0:00 - Introduction ] ──► [ 1:45 - Equation Setup ] ──► [ 4:20 - Shortcut Method ] ──► [ 6:15 - Summary ]
```

### 1. Introduction (`0:00`)
- **Focus**: Problem stem read-through and variable definition.

### 2. Equation Setup (`1:45`)
- **Focus**: Translating text constraints into mathematical algebra.

### 3. Shortcut Method (`4:20`)
- **Focus**: Applying the platform's target mental shortcuts (e.g. 100x base calculations) to solve without decimals.

### 4. Summary (`6:15`)
- **Focus**: Review of trap options and common error paths.

## Best Practices for Video Study

<Callout type="info">
  **Best Practice: Trigger the Shortcut Timestamp First**  
  If you solved a question correctly but took more than 90 seconds, click the `4:20 - Shortcut Method` link to see if the instructor used a faster mental shorthand to complete it under 30 seconds.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning">
  **Warning: Data Overhead**  
  Video streaming consumes bandwidth. If you are preparing on low-bandwidth networks, leverage the **Written Solution** tab or ask the **AI ELI5 Assistant** for text-based step-by-step breakdowns first.
</Callout>
