## Why ELI5 Exists

Standard math explanations rely on dry algebraic equations:
$$\text{Profit} = \text{SP} - \text{CP}$$
This format can be difficult to absorb when learning a concept for the first time. The **AI ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5)** assistant breaks down calculations using simple real-world analogies:
- **Quant Analogy**: Treats profit/loss variables as cookies baked and sold at a school fair.
- **Logical Analogy**: Explains seating arrangements as coordinating seats on a Ferris wheel.
- **Verbal Analogy**: Translates difficult semantic roots into everyday visual metaphors.

## Example ELI5 Breakdown

Here is a mock output generated by the assistant for a hard Profit & Loss problem:

> 🍪 **The Cookie Analogy**  
> Imagine you buy cookies from a bakery for 10 coins. That's your **Cost Price (CP)**. You want to sell them to friends.
> - First, you tell everyone they cost 15 coins. That's your **Markup**.
> - Nobody buys them! So you say: "Okay, 2 coins discount!" That's your **Sales Price (13 coins)**.
> - Your net profit is 3 coins. That's it!
>
> ⚡ **Speed Shortcut**  
> Instead of using variables like $x$ and $y$, set $CP = 100$. Your markup is $150$. A $10\%$ discount on $150$ equals $15$. Your selling price is $135$. Net profit is $35\%$.
>
> 🎓 **TCS NQT Exam Tip**  
> TCS questions often combine successive markup and discount percentages. Using the $100$ base method prevents fraction errors under exam pressure.
